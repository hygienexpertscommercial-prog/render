About Us

Specialist Commercial Kitchen Cleaning Services

HYGIENEXPERTS provides professional commercial kitchen cleaning services for restaurants, cafes, catering businesses, hotels, clubs and other food service facilities throughout Sydney.

Our trained cleaning specialists remove grease, oil, soot and built-up residue from commercial kitchen ventilation systems, cooking equipment, preparation areas, floors and cool rooms.

We understand the importance of maintaining a clean, hygienic and safe commercial kitchen. Our services help support Australian health and hygiene requirements while reducing fire risks caused by grease accumulation.

With 24/7 availability, we can work around your operating hours to minimise disruption to your business.
